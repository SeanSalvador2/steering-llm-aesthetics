# UI-generation benchmarks & arenas (survey cluster, incl. 2025–26)

Landscape of how the field evaluates generated UIs. Confirms (a) objective+judge is the emerging standard, (b) MLLM-as-judge with checklists correlates strongly with human votes, and (c) no one isolates *which design instruction* causes quality — our Stage-1 gap.

## Datasets / screenshot-to-code
- **WebSight — Laurençon et al. 2024 (arXiv:2403.09029):** synthetic screenshot↔HTML dataset; v0.1 = **823k** pairs, v0.2 scaled to **2M** with **Tailwind CSS** + real images; ideas generated by Mistral-7B. Training data, not an eval; relevant as a source of realistic single-file HTML style and as a fine-tune corpus if ever needed.
- **Web2Code — Yun et al. 2024 (arXiv:2406.20098):** large webpage-to-code instruction-tuning dataset + eval framework for MLLM webpage understanding & HTML generation.

## Front-end code-gen benchmarks
- **Design2Code (A1, arXiv:2403.03163):** 484 pages, reference-based block/text/position/color/CLIP metrics.
- **DesignBench — Xiao et al. 2025 (arXiv:2506.06251):** 900 samples, **React/Vue/Angular + vanilla HTML/CSS**, three tasks (generation/edit/repair); multi-dimensional.
- **Vision2Web — He et al. 2026 (arXiv:2603.26648):** hierarchical (static UI→interactive→full-stack), 193 tasks; **GUI-agent verifier + VLM judge** dual evaluation.
- **Interaction2Code / UI2Code^N / ScreenCoder / VisRefiner:** interactive and self-refinement variants (2025–26) — trend toward *executable + interactive* evaluation.

## Visual-interactive / judged benchmarks (most relevant to our oracle)
- **ArtifactsBench — Zhang et al. 2025 (arXiv:2507.04952, Tencent):** **1,825 tasks**; renders each artifact in a sandbox, captures **3 sequential interaction screenshots**, scores with a **checklist-guided MLLM-as-Judge** (per-task 10-dimension checklists). Achieves **94.4% ranking consistency with WebDev Arena** and **>90% pairwise agreement with human experts** — strong evidence that a **rubric-anchored MLLM judge is reliable** (supports ADR-005). Notably: generalist models often beat domain-specific ones.
- **WebDev Arena (LMArena):** live human pairwise voting on web-app generations; the human "gold standard" ArtifactsBench validates against.
- **UI-Bench 2025 (arXiv:2508.20410):** 10 text-to-app tools, 30 prompts, **300 sites, 4,000+ expert pairwise judgments** — expert pairwise comparison of *visual excellence* specifically. Confirms pairwise-expert protocol at our scale is standard.
- **DesignArena (designarena.ai):** crowdsourced arena; **single-file HTML** outputs across Website/UI-component/Game/Dataviz/3D — validates our single-file-HTML constraint (ADR-006) as an accepted format.
- **G-FOCUS / WiserUI-Bench — Jeon et al. 2025 (arXiv:2505.05026):** **pairwise UI persuasiveness** with 300 A/B pairs + expert rationales; introduces an inference-time strategy to **reduce position bias** in VLM pairwise UI judging — directly reusable for our judge protocol.

## Net for Project 19
- **Objective metrics + judge is the field standard**; rubric-anchored MLLM judges reach ~90%+ human agreement (ArtifactsBench) — de-risks ADR-005.
- **Pairwise expert/VLM comparison at thousands of judgments** is normal (UI-Bench, WiserUI-Bench) — our ~2–4k judgments are reasonable.
- **Gap we fill:** every benchmark scores *models/tools*; none **ablates the design instruction** to attribute quality to *components*, and none does **activation-level** analysis. That is Stage 1 (ablation) + Stage 2 (interp) novelty.

## Borrow
- ArtifactsBench **checklist-guided** judging + multi-screenshot capture; report agreement vs a human subset.
- WiserUI-Bench/G-FOCUS **position-bias mitigation** for pairwise VLM judging (both orders + reasoning strategy).
- Single-file HTML as an accepted output format (DesignArena).

## Avoid
- Reference-based metrics as headline (no ground-truth page).
- Trusting an MLLM judge without a human-agreement check on a subset (our reliability gate).
